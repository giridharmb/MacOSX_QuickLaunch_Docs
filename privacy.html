Privacy Policy

Effective October 24, 2026. Short version: QuickLaunch doesn't collect, sell or share any data about you.

What QuickLaunch stores

Your links, shortcuts and settings are saved on your Mac. If you turn on iCloud sync, they are also saved in your own iCloud account, through Apple. The developer can't see them.

Usage counts (how often each link is used, and which apps you bring to the front, for the Insights suggestions) stay on your Mac. They are never synced or sent anywhere. You can clear them in Settings › Advanced.

Network access

To show website icons, QuickLaunch connects directly to websites you add as links and downloads their icon. No third-party icon service is used. The direct-download edition also checks the QuickLaunch website for updates; the App Store edition gets updates from the App Store.

What QuickLaunch does not do

  • No accounts or sign-in.
  • No analytics, tracking, advertising or crash-reporting SDKs.
  • No keystroke logging. QuickLaunch is only told when one of your registered shortcuts is pressed.
  • No data is sold or shared with anyone.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Open an issue. Please don't include personal information; issues are public.
