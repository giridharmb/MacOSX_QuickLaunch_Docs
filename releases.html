Release Notes

What's new in QuickLaunch. Get the latest from the Releases page, or use Check for Updates… in the app.

1.4.1

October 24, 2026
  • Fixed: a big empty gap under the first link in the Manage window.
  • Fixed: Preferences opened narrower than its content, cut off part of the Advanced tab, and showed “Untitled” as its title.
  • Fixed: the Leader key box in the link editor was nearly invisible.
  • The Manage window has a minimum size so rows can't be squeezed, and the Insights sheet is taller.
  • Insights no longer suggests adding QuickLaunch itself.

1.4.0

October 24, 2026
  • Favorites: star links to pin them in a Favorites section at the top of the menu. They also come first in the search launcher and get first pick of leader-key letters.
  • Website icons: URL links show the site's own icon, fetched directly from the site and cached.
  • Custom icons: give any link an image or another app's icon (choose or drag and drop). Custom icons sync through iCloud.
  • Settings › Advanced › Refresh Website Icons.
  • Settings written by older versions keep loading unchanged.

1.3.0

October 24, 2026
  • Preferences redesigned as a classic toolbar window: General, Shortcuts, Sync, Advanced and About. About shows the app icon, version, and links to docs and support.
  • Live shortcut test: after recording, press the shortcut once to confirm it reaches QuickLaunch. If it never arrives, something else is intercepting it. Click any status icon to test again.
  • More conflicts detected: custom App Shortcuts from System Settings and Services keyboard shortcuts, with no permission needed.
  • Fixed: the leader-key overlay could be cut off at the top and bottom.

1.2.0

October 24, 2026
  • Leader key, search launcher and cheat sheet overlays.
  • Workspaces: one shortcut opens several links.
  • Pause shortcuts while chosen apps are in front.
  • Add the frontmost app from the menu bar menu.
  • Insights: usage counts, unused shortcuts, and suggestions for apps you open often by hand.
  • Optional Accessibility features: cycle windows, window placement, app-menu conflict scan.
  • Automatic updates.

1.1.0

October 24, 2026
  • New app icon.
  • Show in menu bar preference.

1.0.0

October 24, 2026
  • First release: global shortcuts for apps, URLs, files, shell commands and Shortcuts, conflict checking, groups, iCloud sync, import and export.
