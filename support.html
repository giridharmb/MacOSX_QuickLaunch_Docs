Troubleshooting

A shortcut does nothing
  1. Open Manage QuickLaunch… and click the shortcut's status icon.
  2. Make sure shortcuts aren't paused. The menu shows Pause All Shortcuts or Paused while … is in front.
  3. Click Test Now and press the shortcut. If the press never arrives, another app or macOS is intercepting it. Choose a different combination; anything with ⌃⌥⌘ is a safe bet.
  4. Check that Test mode is off (Settings › Shortcuts).
The status icon is red: “already registered by another app”

Another running app has claimed that combination. Pick a different one, or remove it in the other app first.

The status icon is orange

The shortcut works, but the same combination is also a macOS shortcut, a custom App Shortcut, a Service, or an app menu item. QuickLaunch takes it over everywhere. Click the icon to see exactly where else it's used.

Window cycling or placement doesn't work

These need the Accessibility permission. Open Settings › Advanced and click Grant Access…, then turn QuickLaunch on in System Settings › Privacy & Security › Accessibility. Some apps, such as full-screen games, don't allow their windows to be moved.

iCloud sync says iCloud Drive isn't enabled

Turn on iCloud Drive in System Settings › Apple Account › iCloud, then turn sync off and on again in QuickLaunch.

I hid the menu bar icon. How do I get back?

Open QuickLaunch again from Finder, Spotlight or Launchpad. The Manage window opens, and your shortcuts keep working the whole time.

macOS says QuickLaunch can't be opened

Download the latest version from the Releases page. If you still see the message, Control-click the app in Finder, choose Open, then confirm.

Where are my settings stored?

~/Library/Application Support/QuickLaunch/config.json, plus the iCloud Drive copy if sync is on. Settings › Advanced › Open Settings Folder takes you there.

When you report a problem

Including these makes a fix much faster:

  • QuickLaunch version (Settings › About) and macOS version
  • The shortcut and what it should open
  • What you expected, and what happened instead
  • Any other launcher or keyboard tools you run (Raycast, Alfred, Karabiner, BetterTouchTool, …)
Please don't post passwords, tokens or other private data in issues. They're public.
